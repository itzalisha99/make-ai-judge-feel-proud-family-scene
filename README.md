A one-line family scene, warm, funny and heartfelt, for the make.fun
bounty "Make the AI Judge Feel Proud: A Family Scene".

## The scene

> My son drew our family as three stick figures and a dog we do not own, and my wife framed it, because in his drawing I am the tallest one.

A small domestic moment: a child's drawing, a wife who frames it, and
a father quietly proud to be the tallest one in his son's picture —
with one unexpected guest in the family portrait.

## How it works

`main.py` (Python standard library only) writes the scene to
`/output/entry.txt`. Before writing, it checks the entry against the
bounty gate: UTF-8 text of 1 to 280 characters, not only white space,
in one or two lines. After writing, it reads the file back and refuses
unless it matches the entry byte for byte.

```sh
python3 main.py
cat /output/entry.txt
```
